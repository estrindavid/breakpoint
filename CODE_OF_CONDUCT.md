# Code of conduct

Breakpoint is a small project. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull
requests, reviews and discussions, is expected to make it a calm, useful place to work.

## What we ask

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, never with people.
- Keep to the subject: the code, the design and the people using the app.
- Give and accept review plainly. A requested change is about the code, not about you.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Do not share anyone's personal details without their consent.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment of any kind; insults, slurs or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, background or
abilities; sexual language or imagery; threats; deliberate disruption; and publishing someone's
private information.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people from the
repository when this code is broken. Decisions are made case by case and are final.

## Reporting

- For something that needs privacy (harassment, a personal attack, anything you would not want to
  discuss in public), use the repository's private reporting route: **Security** tab →
  **Report a vulnerability**, and say in the title that it is a conduct report. Only the maintainer
  sees it.
- For anything that can be discussed openly, open an issue.

This code is written for this project. For a fuller treatment of the same ideas, see the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
